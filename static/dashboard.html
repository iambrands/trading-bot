EMA + RSI + Volume Strategy Guide

Overview

TradePilot uses a confluence-based scalping strategy that combines three key indicators: Exponential Moving Average (EMA), Relative Strength Index (RSI), and Volume. By requiring all three to align, we only take high-quality trades with strong momentum and confirmation.

Entry Conditions

🔵 LONG (Buy) Entry

  • Price > EMA(50): Price must be above the 50-period Exponential Moving Average, indicating an uptrend
  • RSI 55-70: RSI must be in the bullish range (not too weak, not overbought)
  • Volume > 1.5x Average: Current volume must exceed 1.5x the 20-period average, confirming momentum
  • Confidence Score ≥ 70%: Must meet minimum confidence threshold based on indicator strength

🔴 SHORT (Sell) Entry

  • Price < EMA(50): Price must be below the EMA, indicating a downtrend
  • RSI 30-45: RSI must be in the bearish range (not too strong, not oversold)
  • Volume > 1.5x Average: Volume spike confirms the downward momentum
  • Confidence Score ≥ 70%: Must meet minimum confidence threshold

Exit Strategy

  • Take Profit: 0.15% - 0.40% profit target (dynamic based on confidence score)
  • Stop Loss: 0.10% - 0.50% loss limit (dynamic based on confidence score)
  • Time-based Exit: Maximum 10 minutes hold time. Position closes at current price if neither TP nor SL is hit.

Why It Works

This strategy uses confluence - the concept that multiple independent indicators confirming the same signal significantly increases the probability of success. By requiring:

  1. Trend confirmation (EMA) - We only trade with the trend
  2. Momentum confirmation (RSI) - Ensures we're not entering at extremes
  3. Volume confirmation - Confirms the move has real participation

This multi-layered approach filters out weak signals and focuses on high-probability setups.
